Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Pristine Heart Care Clinic, we strive to provide the highest quality medical second opinions through the expertise of Professor Dr. Gajendra Agrawal. Please read our policy regarding cancellations and refunds carefully.

Official Practice Name: Pristine Heart Care Clinic
Service: Digital Medical Second Opinion / Clinical Review

1. Cancellation Policy

Patients may request a cancellation of their consultation request within 2 hours of making the payment, provided that the medical records have not yet been accessed or reviewed by the clinical team. To request a cancellation, please email consult@professoragrawal.com with your transaction details.

2. Non-Refundable Services

Because the service provided is a professional medical consultancy based on expertise and time:

  • Once Professor Dr. Gajendra Agrawal has commenced the review of your clinical history, imaging (Angiograms/Echo), and reports, the fee becomes non-refundable.
  • Refunds will not be issued based on the "nature" of the medical opinion provided (e.g., if the opinion differs from your local physician's advice).

3. Refund Processing

In the event that a refund is approved (e.g., duplicate payment or cancellation within the 2-hour window), the amount will be credited back to the original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, or UPI) via the Razorpay gateway within 5 to 7 working days.

4. Delivery of Service

The "Digital Second Opinion" report is typically delivered within 24-48 hours of successful payment and document upload. If there is a delay beyond 72 hours due to technical issues, the patient may request a full refund.
