A Trusted Second-Opinion Resource for Your Patients

When a patient is facing a major cardiac treatment decision, an independent cardiology review can help the patient and family better understand the available options before proceeding.

Dr. Gajendra Agrawal provides independent cardiology second-opinion reviews based on the clinical history, investigations, imaging and reports submitted by the patient.

The service is designed to complement the patient's existing treating team—not to replace the treating physician, hospital, or emergency medical care.

Why Refer a Patient?

🫀 Independent Clinical Perspective

Your patient can obtain an independent cardiology assessment before making an important treatment decision.

📋 Review of Existing Reports

The review can include ECG, Echo, angiography, CT coronary angiography, laboratory reports and relevant clinical records.

🔎 Treatment Decision Support

The second opinion focuses on helping the patient understand the clinical findings and potential treatment options.

🌍 Convenient International Access

Patients can submit their medical information remotely, without needing to travel solely to obtain an initial independent review.

Which Patients May Benefit?

Referral may be considered when a patient or family is seeking an independent review of a significant cardiac diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

Angioplasty / Stent Recommendation Patients advised to undergo PCI who want an independent review of the proposed treatment.
Bypass Surgery Recommendation Patients advised to undergo CABG who want another cardiology perspective before proceeding.
Complex Coronary Artery Disease Patients with significant or multivessel coronary disease requiring treatment-decision support.
Uncertain or Conflicting Opinions Patients who have received differing opinions regarding their cardiac diagnosis or treatment.
Heart Failure / Cardiomyopathy Patients seeking an independent review of their cardiac evaluation and treatment plan.
Valve or Structural Heart Disease Patients seeking an independent review of their available cardiac investigations and treatment options.
