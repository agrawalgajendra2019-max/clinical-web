Frequently Asked Questions

What medical records should I upload?

Please upload any relevant cardiac reports available with you, such as angiography reports, echocardiography reports, ECGs, discharge summaries, blood investigations, prescriptions, or other related medical records.

How long does the consultation review usually take?

Initial review and response are generally provided within approximately 24–48 hours after submission of records and consultation details.

Is this an emergency medical service?

No. This platform is intended only for non-emergency online cardiology consultation and second-opinion guidance. Patients with severe chest pain, collapse, acute breathlessness, or other medical emergencies should seek immediate local emergency care.

Can international patients request consultation?

International patients may submit consultation requests and medical records for expert review and second-opinion guidance, subject to applicable operational and consultation limitations.

How will the consultation opinion be shared?

Consultation guidance may be shared through written report review, email communication, WhatsApp coordination, or scheduled video consultation depending on the consultation type selected.

Are my medical records kept confidential?

Yes. Medical records and patient information are handled confidentially and used only for consultation and follow-up purposes within the clinical workflow.
