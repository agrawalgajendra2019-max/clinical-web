Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This policy outlines how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

We collect medical records, imaging reports, and contact information specifically for the purpose of providing a medical second opinion.

2. Data Security

All medical data is stored in secure, HIPAA-compliant environments. We do not share your medical history with third parties except for the clinical team involved in your review.

3. Payment Information

We do not store your credit card or banking details. All transactions are processed via Razorpay, a secure, PCI-DSS compliant gateway.
